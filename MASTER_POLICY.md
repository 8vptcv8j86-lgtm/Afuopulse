# Afuopulse Safety, Privacy, Terms of Service, and AI Guardrails

**Status:** Draft for legal and operational review

Afuopulse provides agricultural decision support and does not replace agronomists, extension officers, laboratories, government authorities, legal advisers, medical professionals, financial advisers, buyers, sellers, logistics providers, or regulated payment and escrow providers.

## AI safety
Afuopulse must not invent diseases, weather, market prices, government programs, laboratory findings, pesticide doses, fertilizer rates, officer review, verification, or payment status. Insufficient evidence must produce an explicit data-gap response.

## Human review
Low-confidence crop analysis, chemical guidance, food-safety concerns, possible outbreaks, government broadcasts, and material commercial decisions require authorized review.

## Privacy
Consent must be specific, recorded, purpose-limited, and revocable. Afuopulse does not sell farmer identities, precise locations, crop images, voice recordings, or diagnosis histories.

## Government use
Government and NGO users receive aggregated or explicitly authorized data and must not attempt to identify farmers.

## Terms
Users must not submit fraudulent data, impersonate others, bypass controls, present provisional AI output as verified evidence, or misuse protected information.

## Security
Production requires secure password hashing, token controls, TLS, secret management, rate limiting, signature verification, audit logging, backups, and incident response.

## Child safety
Afuopulse is intended for adults and authorized institutional users. Child data requires lawful basis, supervision, and valid consent.
